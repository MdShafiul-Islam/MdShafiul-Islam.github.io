Welcome to my academic website. I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Economics at the University of Oklahoma. I am also an Associate Professor (On Study Leave) in the Department of Economics at Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur, Bangladesh.

My research lies at the intersection of Development Economics, Applied Econometrics, Industrial Organization, and Energy Economics. My current work examines poverty, food insecurity, education, and public policy, with a particular focus on Bangladesh and other developing countries.
