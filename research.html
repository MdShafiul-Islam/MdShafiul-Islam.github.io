Research

My research is in development economics and applied microeconometrics, with secondary interests in industrial organization and environmental and energy economics. My current work examines social protection targeting, climate and flood shocks, food security, and household welfare in Bangladesh and other developing countries.

Research Interests

  • Development Economics
  • Applied Microeconometrics
  • Industrial Organization
  • Environmental and Energy Economics

Within these fields, my work examines social protection and poverty, climate and flood shocks, food security and nutrition, education, and the evaluation of public policy.
